TadPath Diagnostics
Services we Offer

We offer academic and research pathology support services from interpretation of human histopathology to the application of cutting-edge digital image analysis. We can also supply specialist laboratory equipment. Here are some details but please contact us to discuss your specific needs:

  • AI and Pharmaceutical Studies - We can perform histology slide annotation by an experienced medically qualified pathologist, scoring, grading and quantitation for making high quality training data for artificial intelligence research and pharmaceutical testing histology and we can advise on histology protocols and methods, quantitation and analysis for the histology component of clinical trials and pharmaceutical studies. We do not do clinical diagnostic work at our lab.
  • Wet Lab Facility - In addition to slide annotation by a pathologist we offer a full wet lab histopathology processing, microtomy sectioning and staining service with a large variety of histology stains available in house. We are experts in human tissue pathology but can also process plant tissues.
  • Immunostains and molecular studies - We have excellent links to immunocytochemistry and molecular labs so, while we do not do those tests in house, we can arrange to have them performed on your samples by request.
  • Digital Image Analysis - We have special expertise in digital image analysis, deconvolution microscopy, 3-Dimensional reconstruction (both in microscopy and macroscopic surgical anatomy extracted from CT scans), algorithm development for image analysis, machine vision in pathology and much more. So whether you are doing clinical research or cutting edge tissue science we would be happy to see how we can help you by developing custom solutions to your unique problems. To see some of the stock specialst services we provide, see our Advanced Techniques page.
  • Laboratory instrument supply - We can supply your hospital, clinic or lab with a range of specialist equipment. We specialise in microscopes and microscope camera systems for projection and image capture. MDT projection systems can be supplied and designed to suite your needs and budgets. Other lab equipment can be supplied on request.
